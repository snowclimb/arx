# Laws

This section will contain **Acts of Arx and other generally binding laws** made under the Constitution.

The current Constitution identifies Acts of Arx as subordinate to the Constitution and constitutional amendments, but above regulations, lawful directions and internal departmental procedures.

!!! note "Records being reorganised"
    No law has yet been published in this section under the new Archive structure.

Future laws should use stable identifiers such as **LAW-001**, **LAW-002**, and so on.
