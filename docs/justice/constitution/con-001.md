## ARTICLE 1: PREAMBLE

<p>The people of this nation, with the aid of Oup, crafts this constitution with the hopes and dreams of bettering the country and its people. To build and develop the west continent economically, to be a shining beacon of progress in the world, and to serve as an example to all, they ordain and promulgate this Constitution.</p>

## ARTICLE 2: TERRITORY OF THE STATE

<p class="section"><strong>SECTION A.</strong> The territory of the nation of Arx comprises the terrestrial, aquatic, aerial, and nether domains located within its borders.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION B.</strong> This includes territories that are owned or occupied by Arx no matter how far away from the mainland.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION C.</strong> Arx has jurisdiction and sovereignty over these areas, including the subsurface, the seabed, and the waters internally within its border regardless of size and dimension.</p>

## ARTICLE 3: PRINCIPLES

<p class="section"><strong>SECTION A.</strong> The Nation of Arx is a Technocratic State, founded upon merit, expertise, innovation, and scientific advancement. Public office exists to promote the efficient administration and long-term prosperity of the Nation.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION B.</strong> The nation of Arx shall adopt any widely accepted international laws and work with other nations that adheres to these same policies.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION C.</strong> The Arx Defense Forces are the protectors of the people and the State. Their goal is to secure the sovereignty of the State and the integrity of the territory.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION D.</strong> The primary duty of the Departments, the Departmental Council and the Praetorian Senate is to serve and protect the Nation and its Citizens while ensuring the continued prosperity, stability and welfare of Arx.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION E.</strong> The nation of Arx will maintain peace and order, the protection of life, liberty, and property, and the promotion of general welfare for the enjoyment of all that lives within its borders.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION F.</strong> Executive authority shall be exercised collectively by the Heads of Departments. Each Head shall govern within their assigned field while cooperating in the administration of the Nation.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION G.</strong> The Head of Justice shall preside over government procedure, minor government decisions, mediation and lawful review. The Head of Justice is not sovereign over the Departmental Council and may be overruled in accordance with this Constitution.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION H.</strong> The order of the legal authority shall be: this Constitution; constitutional amendments; Acts of Arx; regulations and lawful directions; and internal departmental procedures. A lower instrument inconsistent with a higher instrument shall be invalid to the extent of said inconsistency.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION I.</strong> Government decisions shall be founded upon evidence, practicality, relevant expertise, proportionality, and the long term interests of Arx.</p>

## ARTICLE 4: CITIZENSHIP & RESIDENCY

<p class="section"><strong>SECTION A.</strong> The following are considered as citizens of the nation of Arx:</p>
<p class="section"><strong>I.</strong> Those who are citizens of Arx at the time of the adoption of this Constitution;</p>
<p class="section"><strong>II.</strong> Residents who are granted Arxian Citizenship in the manner provided by law, with evidence recorded through official ticketing systems;</p>
<p class="section"><strong>III.</strong> A resident can be directly promoted to a Citizen through the lawful citizenship procedure or delegated approval path.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION B.</strong> Arxian citizenship may be lost or reacquired in the manner provided by law and with appropriate procedural fairness.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION C.</strong> Citizens who marry or otherwise associate with foreigners shall retain Arxian Citizenship unless they lawfully renounce it or are denaturalised by due process.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION D.</strong> Dual allegiance of citizens is allowed. A dual citizen alleged to have leaked protected information, transferred protected goods, or otherwise acted seriously against Arx shall be investigated and may be punished or denaturalised after notice, opportunity to respond and an impartial determination proportionate to the seriousness of conduct.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION E.</strong> Citizens may recruit Residents and assist in providing Namelayer or settlement access, provided that the Resident files the required ticket and receives approval through authorised procedure.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION F.</strong> Any person facing serious sanction, loss of office, removal of access, or denaturalisation shall be informed of the allegation, given reasonable opportunity to respond, and permit an appeal or review where provided by law.</p>

## ARTICLE 5: PRAETORSHIP AND THE PRAETORIAN SENATE

<p class="section"><strong>SECTION A.</strong> The collective body of all serving Praetors shall be known as the Praetorian Senate.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION B.</strong> Praetorship shall be granted only to Citizens who have demonstrated exceptional service, expertise, judgement, leadership, trust or contribution to Arx, in the manner provided by law.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION C.</strong> Praetors are eligible to hold positions of importance in government. Praetorship doesn’t itself create a Departmental office.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION D.</strong> A Praetor may relinquish their government office or Praetorship the manner provided by law. Relinquishing a government office does not automatically remove Praetorship unless expressly stated.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION E.</strong> The Praetorian Senate shall:</p>
<p class="section"><strong>I.</strong> Confirm Department Heads through the voting procedure established by this Constitution;</p>
<p class="section"><strong>II.</strong> Review tied or lawfully referred Department Council decisions;</p>
<p class="section"><strong>III.</strong> Remove an ordinary Department Head by majority vote after giving that Head a reasonable opportunity to respond;</p>
<p class="section"><strong>IV.</strong> Raise matters to the Head of Justice by majority vote;</p>
<p class="section"><strong>V.</strong> Require the reconsideration of a government matter by majority vote where material evidence, procedure, or consequences justify review;</p>
<p class="section"><strong>VI.</strong> Participate in constitutional, territorial, diplomatic, and national decisions assigned to it by these conditions;</p>
<p class="section"><strong>VII.</strong> Periodically review the performance, activity, and suitability of Department Heads.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION F.</strong> A Praetorian vote shall be decided by a majority of valid votes cast unless this Constitution requires a greater threshold. Failure of eligible Praetors to vote within the prescribed period shall not invalidate the result.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION G.</strong> Praetorian ballots shall be secret. Aggregate totals, abstentions, and final result shall be recorded.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION H.</strong> Praetors that remain inactive shall be termed Praetors Emeritus and hold no power, A Praetor Emeritus is given back his power after they demonstrate that they can be active again.</p>

## ARTICLE 6: SUFFRAGE

<p class="section"><strong>SECTION A.</strong> Suffrage may be practiced only by citizens of Arx. No literacy, property, or other substantive requirement shall be imposed on the exercise of suffrage.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION B.</strong> Citizen and Praetorian votes shall be decided by majority of valid votes cast unless this constitution expressly requires a greater threshold. No participation quorum shall apply to Citizen or Praetorian votes. Failure by an eligible person to vote within the prescribed voting period shall not invalidate the result.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION C.</strong> A binding Citizen vote shall be required for:</p>
<p class="section"><strong>I.</strong> Confirmation of a Department Head;</p>
<p class="section"><strong>II.</strong> Final ratification of the removal of the Head of Justice;</p>
<p class="section"><strong>III.</strong> A merger of dissolution of Arx;</p>
<p class="section"><strong>IV.</strong> Permanent transfer of the principal territory of Arx;</p>
<p class="section"><strong>V.</strong> A constitutional amendment materially affecting Citizen rights, the fundamental structure of government, or national sovereignty;</p>
<p class="section"><strong>VI.</strong> A change to the official name of the nation;</p>
<p class="section"><strong>VII.</strong> Any other matter expressly referred to Citizens under this Constitution or by lawful joint decision of the Departmental Council and Praetorian Senate.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION D.</strong> The Government may conduct advisory Citizen polls on appointments, public works, culture, planning or other matters. An advisory poll shall inform but not bind lawful decision-maker unless the proposal states otherwise before voting begins.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION E.</strong> Elections, confirmation votes, removal votes, and public referendums shall ordinarily remain open for seventy-two hours and conducted by secret ballots. Aggregate totals and results shall be published.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION F.</strong> Where one candidate stands for a Department Head office, Citizens, and Praetors shall vote separately to:</p>
<p class="section"><strong>I.</strong> Approval;</p>
<p class="section"><strong>II.</strong> Reject;</p>
<p class="section"><strong>III.</strong> Abstain; The candidate shall be appointed only when Approve votes exceed Reject votes in both Citizen electorate and Praetorian electorate. Abstentions shall not count as either Approve or Reject.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION G.</strong> Where two or more candidates stand for appointment as a Department Head, the election shall be conducted separately among Citizens and Praetors using the single-seat form of the Single Transferable Vote. Each voter may rank the candidates in order of preference by marking a first preference, second preference, third preference, and any further preferences they wish to express. A voter shall not be required to rank every candidate.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION H.</strong> Votes cast under the Single Transferable Vote shall be counted as follows:</p>
<p class="section"><strong>I.</strong> Each valid ballot shall initially be allocated to a continuing candidate ranked as the voter’s first preference.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>II.</strong> A candidate receiving more than one-half of valid continuing ballots, said candidate shall be declared the winner of that electorate.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>III.</strong> Where no candidate receives more than one-half of the valid continuing ballots, the candidate with the fewest votes shall be eliminated.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>IV.</strong> Each ballot allocated to an eliminated candidate shall be transferred at full value to the next-ranked candidate who remains in the election.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>V.</strong> A ballot containing no further preference for a continuing candidate shall become exhausted and shall not be counted in later rounds.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>VI.</strong> After each elimination and transfer, the votes shall be recounted.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>VII.</strong> The elimination and transfer process shall continue until one candidate receives more than one-half of the valid continuing ballots.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>VIII.</strong> Citizen ballots and Praetorian ballots shall be counted separately, and a separate winner shall be determined for each electorate.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION I.</strong> Where candidates are tied for the fewest votes during an elimination round:</p>
<p class="section"><strong>I.</strong> The candidate who received fewer votes in the immediately preceding round shall be eliminated.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>II.</strong> Where the candidates were also tied in the preceding round, earlier rounds shall be considered in reverse order until the tie can be resolved.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>III.</strong> </p>
<p>Where no earlier round resolves the tie, a separate tied-candidate ballot shall be held among the relevant electorate; and</p>
<p class="section"><strong>IV.</strong> Where the tie remains unresolved, the Head of Justice shall supervise an impartial random selection to determine which tied candidate is eliminated. Where the Head of Justice is a candidate, is conflicted, or is otherwise unable to supervise the procedure, the Deputy Presiding Judge or another impartial Praetor lawfully appointed for the purpose shall supervise it.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION J.</strong> A candidate shall be appointed as Department Head if he is competent in the field and where the same candidate wins both:</p>
<p class="section"><strong>I.</strong> The Citizen electorate;</p>
<p class="section"><strong>II.</strong> The Praetorian electorate. Neither electorate shall take priority over the other.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION K.</strong> Where the Citizen electorate and Praetorian electorate select different candidates:</p>
<p class="section"><strong>I.</strong> A final runoff selection shall be held between the two candidates selected by the respective electorates;</p>
<p class="section"><strong>II.</strong> Citizens and Praetors shall again vote as separate electorates;</p>
<p class="section"><strong>III.</strong> The runoff shall use preferential ballots under the single-seat Single Transferable Vote procedure;</p>
<p class="section"><strong>IV.</strong> The runoff shall ordinarily remain open for seventy-two hours; and</p>
<p class="section"><strong>V.</strong> A candidate shall be appointed only where they receive majority support in both electorates Where neither candidate wins both electorates, appointments shall remain vacant and nominations shall reopen.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION L.</strong> A blank ballot or a ballot that does not clearly express any valid preference shall not be counted. A ballot shall not be invalid merely because the voter has ranked only some of the available candidates.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION M.</strong> Detailed ballot formats, counting procedures, Discord bot functions and election records may be established through lawful government procedure, provided they remain consistent with this article.</p>

## ARTICLE 7: DEPARTMENTS AND THE DEPARTMENTAL COUNCIL

<p class="section"><strong>SECTION A.</strong> The Departments are:</p>
<p class="section"><strong>I.</strong> Department of Culture;</p>
<p class="section"><strong>II.</strong> Department of Infrastructure;</p>
<p class="section"><strong>III.</strong> Department of Defense;</p>
<p class="section"><strong>IV.</strong> Department of Justice;</p>
<p class="section"><strong>V.</strong> Department of Economy;</p>
<p class="section"><strong>VI.</strong> Department of Technology;</p>
<p class="section"><strong>VII.</strong> Department of Architecture.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION B.</strong> Eligibility, candidacy, confirmation and appointment of Department Heads shall be governed by Articles 6 and 8 of this Constitution.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION C.</strong> Each Department shall be headed by a Department Head who should be competent in his field. Appointment as a Department Head automatically creates membership of the Departmental Council. There shall be no separate office of Councillor.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION D.</strong> The Departmental Council is the collective government of Arx and shall decide significant national, cross-departmental and strategic matters</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION E.</strong> A Department Head is permitted to select and appoint up to two managers who aid the Department Head. In case the Head has to be away for some time they need to appoint someone to perform their duties</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION F.</strong> The quorum of Department Council shall be at least one-half of currently serving and eligible Department Heads, rounded upward. Vacant offices, suspended Heads, and heads recuse themselves shall be excluded when calculating quorum for the relevant matter. The Council should seek the participation of all eligible Heads wherever reasonably possible.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION G.</strong> An ordinary Council decision shall be determined by a simple majority of valid votes cast. Abstentions shall not count as Yes or No. A tied substantive vote shall be referred automatically to the Praetorian Senate.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION H.</strong> Ordinary Departmental Council polls shall remain open for twenty-four hours or until every eligible Head was voted, whichever occurs first.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION I.</strong> Each formal Council matter shall ordinarily contain:</p>
<p class="section"><strong>I.</strong> A ballot asking whether the proposed action shall be approved, with Yes, No, and Abstain;</p>
<p class="section"><strong>II.</strong> A separate ballot asking whether the matter shall be referred to the Praetorian Senate for final determination.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION J.</strong> Where the referral ballot passes by simple majority, the substantive Council result shall be provisional and the decision of the Praetorian Senate shall be binding. A tied referral ballot shall also result in referral.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION K.</strong> No person may cast more than one vote in the same Departmental Council poll, even if said person is in temporary holding of more than one office.</p>

## ARTICLE 8: DEPARTMENT HEADS AND APPOINTMENT

<p class="section"><strong>SECTION A.</strong> A Department Head shall be a Praetor upon taking office and shall possess demonstrated competence, experience, or contribution relevant to the jurisdiction of the Department.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION B.</strong> In keeping with the technocratic principles of Arx, preference should be given to candidates who:</p>
<p class="section"><strong>I.</strong> Possess substantial experience within the relevant Department;</p>
<p class="section"><strong>II.</strong> </p>
<p class="section"><strong>III.</strong> </p>
<p class="section"><strong>IV.</strong> </p>
<p>Have previously served as a Manager, Member, or significant contributor within that Department; Possess suitable knowledge or practical skill revant to said Department’s field; Have demonstrated sustained interest, commitment and genuine enthusiasm for work of said Department.</p>
<p>A candidate should seek office primarily to advance the work, responsibilities, and development of the Department and not solely to obtain membership of the Departmental Council or wider governmental authority. A lack of previous service within the Department shall not automatically disqualify a candidate provided they are able to demonstrate sufficient knowledge, commitment, and capacity to lead the Department effectively.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION C.</strong> Any serving Praetor may stand for appointment as a Department Head.</p>
<p>A Citizen who is not yet Praetor may also stand, provided that their appointment shall not take effect unless they are granted Praetorship before or alongside their confirmation as Department Head. A Citizen candidate may therefore undergo the Praetorship and Department Head confirmation procedures during the same election period.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION D.</strong> Before voting begins, every candidate shall provide a concise statement setting out:</p>
<p class="section"><strong>I.</strong> Their relevant knowledge, competence, and experience;</p>
<p class="section"><strong>II.</strong> Any previous service as a Manager, Member, or contributor within the Department;</p>
<p class="section"><strong>III.</strong> Their interest in and commitment to the Department’s field;</p>
<p class="section"><strong>IV.</strong> Their intended priorities for the Department;</p>
<p class="section"><strong>V.</strong> Any known conflict of interest relevant to the office;</p>
<p class="section"><strong>VI.</strong> Their willingness and expected ability to remain active in the role.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION E.</strong> Appointment as a Department Head shall require separate majority support from:</p>
<p class="section"><strong>I.</strong> Participating citizens;</p>
<p class="section"><strong>II.</strong> Participating members of the Praetorian Senate. Where only one candidate stands, Citizens and Praetors shall vote separately to Approve, Reject or Abstain. The candidate shall be appointed only where Approve votes exceed Reject votes in both electorates. Where two or more candidates stand, the single-seat form of the Single Transferable Vote established under Article 6 shall apply separately to the Citizen electorate and Praetorian electorate. A candidate shall not take office unless the same candidate succeeds in both electorates and satisfies all eligibility requirements. Where the Citizen electorate and the Praetorian electorate select different candidates, a final runoff shall be conducted in accordance with Article 6.</p>
<p>Where a Citizen candidate succeeds in both electorates but is not granted Praetorship, the appointment shall fail and the office shall remain vacant until another lawful appointment is completed.</p>

## ARTICLE 9: JUSTICE, GOVERNMENT PROCEDURE AND REVIEW

<p class="section"><strong>SECTION A.</strong> The Head of Justice shall serve as presiding Judge of government procedure and shall chair the Department Council, administer minor government decisions, mediate disputes, oversee lawful procedure, and coordinate matters assigned to Justice.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION B.</strong> Government matters shall be classified as follows:</p>
<p class="section"><strong>I.</strong> Departmental matters - a matter wholly within one Department, within approved resources and without material effect upon Department or national policy;</p>
<p class="section"><strong>II.</strong> Minor government matter - a limited administrative, scheduling, coordination or cross-departmental question not involving major expenditure, rights, territory, or serious foreign policy;</p>
<p class="section"><strong>III.</strong> Departmental Council matter - a significant cross-departmental policy regarding a strategic, financial, security or foreign matter;</p>
<p class="section"><strong>IV.</strong> Praetorian or constitutional matter - a tied, referred, contested or fundamental matter assigned to the Praetorian Senate or Citizens;</p>
<p class="section"><strong>V.</strong> Emergency matter - a matter requiring immediate temporary action to prevent serious harm, loss, security compromise or interruption of essential functions.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION C.</strong> A Department Head shall decide Departmental matters. The Head of Justice shall decide Minor government matters after consulting relevant Heads where reasonably appropriate. The Departmental Council shall decide Council matters.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION D.</strong> The Head of Justice shall make the initial classification of a contested matter. The Departmental Council may change that classification or overturn a Justice ruling by simple majority.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION E.</strong> Where two Departments are unable to agree upon responsibility, access, priority or procedure Justice shall mediate and can issue a ruling. A materially affected Head may request Council review.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION F.</strong> By majority vote, the Praetorian Senate may raise a matter to Justice. Where the matter concerns one or more Department heads, Justice may begin review, request information, preserve relevant records, temporarily pause the disputed action or require a formal vote without prior approval from those Heads.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION G.</strong> Justice acting under Section F shall not permanently remove a Head, size another Department, approve major expenditure, or alter national policy without the approvals overwise required by this constitution.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION H.</strong> By majority vote, the Praetorian Senate may issue a Motion for Reconsideration requiring Justice or the Departmental Council to readdress a decision. A settled matter should not be reopened more than once unless new evidence emerges, circumstances materially change, or the original procedure was unlawful.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION I.</strong> The Head of Justice shall recuse themselves from a matter where they are personally involved, directly interested, where the Department of Justice is a principal party and impartially may reasonably be questioned.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION J.</strong> The Head of Justice shall nominate a Deputy Presiding Judge from among eligible Praetors, subject to confirmation by simple majority of the Departmental council. The Deputy shall remain in office until resignation, replacement, or lawful removal.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION K.</strong> The Deputy Presiding Judge shall exercise the procedural authority of Justice during absence, recusal, incapacity, or suspension, but shall not automatically become permanent Head of the Department of Justice.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION L.</strong> The Deputy may be removed by simple majority of either the Departmental Council or the Praetorian Senate. Where both Justice and the Deputy are unavailable or conflicted, the Council shall elect a temporary presiding Praetor. Where the Council collectively is the subject of the matter, the Praetorian Senate shall appoint the temporary presiding person.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION M.</strong> The Departmental Council may temporarily suspend the Head of Justice only by a simple majority of the other eligible Department Heads and a separate simple majority of eligible Praetors. During suspension, the Deputy shall act.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION N.</strong> Permanent removal of the Head of Justice shall require, in order:</p>
<p class="section"><strong>I.</strong> A simple majority vote of the other eligible Department Heads;</p>
<p class="section"><strong>II.</strong> A separate simple majority vote of eligible Praetors; and</p>
<p class="section"><strong>III.</strong> A final binding simple majority vote of participating Citizens.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION O.</strong> The Head of Justice shall not vote in any poll concerning their own suspension or removal and shall be given a reasonable opportunity to respond before final determination.</p>

## ARTICLE 10: FINANCE, TREASURY, AND STATE RESOURCES

<p class="section"><strong>SECTION A.</strong> The Department of Economy shall maintain and account for the national treasury, state exchanges, approved government shops, valuations, and financial procedures.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION B.</strong> The Department of Economy shall determine and periodically review the delegated project spending limit, subject to simple majority approval of the Departmental Council.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION C.</strong> The initial delegated project spending limit shall be one hundred diamonds, or resources and assets of equivalent value as determined under the valuation procedure maintained by the Department of Economy.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION D.</strong> Any increase in the delegated limit, being a change that makes financial control less restrictive, shall additionally require approval of the Head of Justice. Justice shall consider procedure, conflicts of interest, anti-corruption concerns, and the proper purpose of the change, and shall provide a brief reason for any refusal.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION E.</strong> A refusal by Justice under Section D may be referred to the Praetorian Senate for final review. A reduction in the limit shall require Economy proposal and Council approval but shall not require additional Justice approval.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION F.</strong> The project limit applies to the total value of a connected project or course of action including all related purchases, withdrawals, transferts, contracts, and resource commitments by every participating Department.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION G.</strong> No expenditure may be divided, delayed, reclassified, or assigned among separate Departments for the purpose of avoiding project spending limits.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION H.</strong> A Department head may authorise ordinary expenditure within the project limit and within the Department’s lawful remit. Expenditure above the limit requires prior Departmental Council approval.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION I.</strong> Emergency expenditure above the limit may be authorised by the relevant Head with Justice approval where reasonably possible, where delay would cause serious loss, security risk, or interruption to essential infrastructure. The expenditure shall be recorded immediately and reviewed by the council within twenty-four hours or as soon as reasonably possible.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION J.</strong> Every state expenditure or transfer shall be entered in a Treasury Log stating the date, Department, amount of value, purpose, authorising person and Council approval reference where required.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION K.</strong> A person with a direct personal interest in a proposed expenditure shall declare that interest and shall not decide or vote upon the matter except to provide relevant factual information.</p>

## ARTICLE 11: FOREIGN AFFAIRS, DEFENCE AND EMERGENCY ACTION

<p class="section"><strong>SECTION A.</strong> The Head of Justice shall oversee diplomatic relations and may appoint Citizens or Praetors as diplomatic representatives, envoys or delegates.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION B.</strong> A diplomatic representative may communicate established Arx policy, attend discussions, gather information and negotiate proposals within an approved mandate. A representative may not independently bind Arx to an alliance, declaration of war, merger, territorial transfer, major resource commitment, or alteration of citizenship and access policy.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION C.</strong> Significant foreign-policy decisions shall be submitted to the Departmental Council. A merger, dissolution or permanent transfer of the principal territory shall additionally require separate majority approval of the Praetorian Senate and participating Citizens.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION D.</strong> The Head of Defense may take immediate and proportionate action to defend Arx, secure compromised access, preserve military or state property, protect Citizens and respond to an active attack or credible imminent threat.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION E.</strong> Emergency action shall be temporary, shall be reported to Justice and the Departmental Council as soon as reasonably possible and shall be reviewed within twenty-four hours where the emergency continues.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION F.</strong> Emergency authority shall not be used to amend this Constitution, permanently remove an office-holder, permanently transfer territory or evade a required vote.</p>

## ARTICLE 12: GENERAL PROVISIONS

<p class="section"><strong>SECTION A.</strong> The flag of the Technocratic State of Arx shall be white and green, with a shield and gear, as consecrated and honored by the people and recognized by law.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION B.</strong> Praetorian Senate collectively may, by law, adopt a new name for the nation, a national anthem, or a national seal, which shall all be truly reflective and symbolic of the ideals, history, and traditions of the people. Such law shall take effect only upon its ratification by the people in a national referendum.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION C.</strong> The nation may not be sued without its consent.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION D.</strong> The State shall provide immediate and adequate care, benefits, and other forms of assistance to war veterans and veterans of military campaigns.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION E.</strong> The State shall protect consumers from trade malpractices and from substandard or hazardous products.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION F.</strong> The ownership and management of mass media shall be limited to citizens of Arx, or to corporations, cooperatives or associations, wholly-owned and managed by such citizens.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION G.</strong> Official government records may be maintained through approved Discord channels, ticketing systems, documents, bots, in-game books or other systems designated by lawful procedure.</p>

## ARTICLE 13: AMENDMENTS OR REVISIONS

<p class="section"><strong>SECTION A.</strong> An amendment or revision may be proposed by any Department Head or member of the Praetorian Senate.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION B.</strong> An amendment shall require three-fourths approval of valid votes cast by the Departmental Council and three-fourths approval of valid votes cast by the Praetorian Senate. The Council quorum in Article 7 shall apply; no turnout quorum shall apply to the Praetorian Senate.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION C.</strong> An amendment materially affecting Citizen rights, the fundamental structure of government, national sovereignty, merger, dissolution or permanent transfer of the principal territory shall additionally require simple majority approval of participating Citizens.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION D.</strong> Amendment ballots shall ordinarily remain open for seventy-two hours. The adopted text and voting totals shall be published before the amendment takes effect.</p>

## ARTICLE 14: TRANSITION AND COMMENCEMENT

<p class="section"><strong>SECTION A.</strong> Upon commencement of this Constitution, the former Central Council shall cease to exist as a separate governmental office. All serving Department Heads shall collectively form the Departmental Council, and there shall be no separate office of Councillor.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION B.</strong> Existing lawful decisions, permissions, appointments, debts, holdings and records shall continue unless amended or repealed under the new system.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION C.</strong> The Departmental Council and Praetorian Senate may adopt temporary arrangements for vacancies, acting appointments, transferred records and ongoing projects during implementation.</p>
<p class="section"><strong>SECTION D.</strong> This Constitution shall take effect upon ratification through the procedure adopted for its approval by Arx.</p>
