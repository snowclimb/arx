# Archive

This section retains superseded, repealed and historical documents so that Arx's legal and governmental history remains traceable.

Archived material should always be clearly labelled so it cannot be mistaken for current law or policy.

## Status terminology

**Superseded** — replaced by a newer version or instrument.  
**Repealed** — formally revoked and no longer in force.  
**Historical** — retained for record but not part of the current legal framework.
