CivMC changes several vanilla transport mechanics. Minecarts can travel faster than vanilla, copper beneath rails can increase their maximum speed, horses have a higher maximum speed, and elytras are disabled.
## Copper rails

Copper blocks beneath rails can raise minecart speed, but the copper can oxidise through use and reduce the maximum speed. By right-clicking the rail with an axe you can remove oxidation from the copper below.

## Arx travel

Use established public rail and ice-road infrastructure where possible instead of cutting new routes without checking where they should connect. Transport networks often cross reinforced or monitored infrastructure.
