CivMC is a player-run civilisation server. Nations, politics, trade, infrastructure, security and most justice are organised by players rather than protected by town or faction systems. That freedom makes this server unusually social, but also means it's important to understand its custom mechanics.

## Your first priorities

1. **Join Arx's Discord** so people can meet you, help you travel and explain local arrangements.
2. **Learn NameLayer** before you start sharing property or permissions.
3. **Learn Citadel** before building anything you wish to preserve; reinforcement is the basic way property is protected.
4. **Understand JukeAlert** so you know how nations monitor important areas.
5. **Understand ExilePearl** because it is central to player-enforced justice.
6. Learn FactoryMod, ItemExchange, Realistic Biomes and mining once you are settled.

We also recommend joining the CivMC Official Discord and using `/discordauth` to verify your Minecraft account there.

## Getting to Arx

New players should contact Arx before travelling blindly. Use the **Join Us** page to enter the Arx Discord, then ask for directions or help reaching the nation.

## Learn in order

This guide library is smaller than the official CivMC Wiki. It focuses on the mechanics most useful to someone living in Arx. Use the **Next guide** button to work through the recommended sequence, and use the source link on each page if you want further information about a given mechanic.
