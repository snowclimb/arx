HiddenOre changes how mining works. Breaking suitable blocks can cause ores to appear around the player, with results influenced by depth. Ore veins can also be discovered and mined out over a much larger area than a normal Minecraft ore cluster.

## Veins

When a vein is discovered, chat messages help indicate new discoveries. The official guide recommends expanding around discovered vein ores rather than assuming the visible block is the whole deposit.

## Tools and processing

The official guide notes that tool choice can affect ore discovery and recommends processing some silk-touched ores through CivMC factory infrastructure for better returns than ordinary vanilla processing.

If you are new, ask an experienced miner before spending hours strip-mining with a vanilla strategy.
