ExilePearl is the mechanic CivMC uses for player imprisonment. A pearled player is exiled to the Nether until the pearl is released or otherwise removed from captivity under the mechanic.

## What you need to understand

Pearling is not a normal Minecraft death. It creates an ongoing state that can be used by nations to enforce sentences, settle conflicts or detain hostile players.

`/ep showall` allows you to view all pearled players and `/ep broadcast [group]` broadcasts a pearl's location to a NameLayer group.

## Arx and pearling

Arx has its own legal and procedural documents governing arrests and criminal matters. The Justice Archive documents when Arxian law determines its use lawful within Arx.
