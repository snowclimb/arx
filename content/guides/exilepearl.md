ExilePearl is the mechanic CivMC uses for player imprisonment. A pearled player is exiled to the Nether until the pearl is released or otherwise removed from captivity under the mechanic.

## What new players need to understand

Pearling is not a normal Minecraft death. It creates an ongoing state that can be used by nations to enforce sentences, settle conflicts or detain hostile players.

The official guide documents `/ep showall` for viewing pearled players and `/ep broadcast [group]` for broadcasting a pearl's location to a NameLayer group.

## Arx and pearling

Arx has its own legal and procedural documents governing arrests and criminal matters. The Justice Archive should be treated separately from this mechanics guide: this page explains what the server mechanic does, while Arxian law determines when Arx considers its use lawful.
