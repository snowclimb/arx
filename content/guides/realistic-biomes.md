Realistic Biomes changes farming so crops and trees grow only in suitable biomes and may take many real-world hours to mature. The mechanic makes geography and trade matter rather than allowing every crop to be produced efficiently everywhere.

## Check your location

Use `/rb` to see what can grow at your current location and the expected growth time.

There are also greenhouse effects from artificial lighting and a **clay soil bonus** that can reduce growth time when correctly built beneath farmland.

## Why you should care

Do not assume a vanilla farm design will work simply because the blocks are placed correctly. Check `/rb` before investing in a large farm, and consider whether an existing Arx farm already produces the crop you need.
