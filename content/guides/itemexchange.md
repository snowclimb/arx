ItemExchange is CivMC's automated chest-trading mechanic. An exchange chest contains encoded exchange rules that define what a buyer provides and what the chest returns.

## Why it matters

This is the basis of many unattended shops. It lets a seller define a fixed rate and lets another player trade without both people being online at the same time.

Exchange chests should be protected appropriately with NameLayer and Citadel. Before setting up a public shop in Arx, check local commercial or building expectations and make sure the chest is stocked with the output it promises.
