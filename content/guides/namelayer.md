NameLayer is CivMC's group and permission system. Other mechanics, especially Citadel and JukeAlert, use NameLayer groups to decide who can access or manage protected infrastructure.

## What a group does

A group can contain multiple players at different permission levels. It can be used for shared chat, shared reinforced property and access to other group-based mechanics.

For a newcomer, the simplest interface is `/nl`, which opens the general NameLayer interface.

## Useful commands

- `/nl` — view and manage the groups you belong to.
- `/nlcg [group name]` — create a group.
- `/nlip [group name] [player] [rank]` — invite a player at a chosen rank.
- `/nlrm [group name] [player]` — remove a player from a group.

## Arx advice

Do not create a new group for every small task before checking whether Arx already has a suitable group. Government, resident and infrastructure access may use established groups. Ask before granting elevated permissions to somebody else.
